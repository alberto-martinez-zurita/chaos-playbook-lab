## Why Apache-2.0 for this artifact

This repository is the public reproducibility bundle for the
**ISSRE 2027 chaos paper** (Chaos Playbook Lab, 2026). Apache-2.0 was chosen for:

- **Permissive use** — researchers and practitioners can build on the artifact
  freely, including in proprietary systems.
- **Explicit patent grant** — the patent clause (§3) protects downstream users
  from patent litigation by contributors. Required by IEEE/ACM reproducibility
  guidelines for academic artifacts that may incorporate novel methods.
- **Compatible with most other OSS licenses** — including MIT, BSD, LGPL.
